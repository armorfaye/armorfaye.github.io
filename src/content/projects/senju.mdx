## The idea

Most computer-use agents borrow your machine. They take the mouse and the
keyboard, and you wait. Senju runs **many agents at once next to you**. Each
one has its own cursor and its own window, and you keep working the whole time.

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd>, type a goal, and a swarm goes
to work. And the swarm **improves itself**: every job it finishes leaves behind
a skill that makes the next one faster and more reliable.

## A self-improving swarm

Senju learns from its own work, without any retraining:

1. **Retrospective.** When a job ends, the lead agent reviews what happened:
   which steps worked, where workers got stuck, and what it had to replan.
2. **Skill.** It saves those lessons as a **skill**, a plain Markdown file you
   can read, edit, or delete.
3. **Reuse.** The next similar job starts from that skill, so the lead plans
   with what the swarm already knows instead of rediscovering it.

Every job adds to the swarm’s skills, so the more you use it, the better it
gets at your work. Because skills are ordinary files, the improvement stays
inspectable: you can see exactly what it learned and correct it. In a team,
skills are shared across everyone’s Macs and every revision is kept.

## How it works

### Lead and workers

- **Lead agent.** Plans the job, spawns workers, waits for their events, and
  replans or stops them when something changes.
- **Workers.** Each worker runs its own Claude tool loop over MCP. Workers can
  hand a step back to you, wait on one another, flag a change of plan, and post
  to a shared board.
- **Ghost cursors.** A transparent overlay draws a cursor for every agent, so
  you can see who is doing what without losing control of your own.

### Tools via MCP

Workers act through Model Context Protocol servers:
**Playwright** for the browser, a **filesystem** server, and **Cua Driver** for
native desktop apps.

### You stay in charge

- **Interruptible.** Message the lead or any worker mid-task and the swarm
  replans. Pause an agent, take over its window, and hand it back.
- **Approval gates.** Sign-ins, and anything sent in your name, wait for your
  click.
- **Your key, your Mac.** Agents run on your own Anthropic API key, which stays
  on your machine.

## Multiplayer

In team mode, one agent can read on your Mac and write on a teammate’s. Its
cursor docks on every screen while it rests and moves to whichever screen it is
called to. Each owner grants permission per session, requests only go to the
person they concern, and shared knowledge is credited to its source.

## Under the hood

- Long-running jobs use context compaction and a token budget.
- Every job writes traces: a readable timeline plus per-agent JSONL logs.
- A demo mode runs without an API key.
